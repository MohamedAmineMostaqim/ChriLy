# Power BI Dashboard — ChriLy

## 📊 Overview

The ChriLy Power BI dashboard is the Business Intelligence layer of the project.

It transforms the PostgreSQL analytical data into an interactive three-page dashboard covering:

- Executive business performance
- Logistics and delivery performance
- Customer and payment behavior
- Geographic performance
- Customer satisfaction

The dashboard was designed to provide both **high-level executive visibility** and **detailed operational analysis**.

---

## 📄 Dashboard Structure

The dashboard contains three pages:

| Page       | Focus                             |
|------------|------------------------------------|
| **Page 1** | Executive Overview                |
| **Page 2** | Logistics & Delivery Performance  |
| **Page 3** | Customer & Payment Insights       |

The three-page structure was intentionally kept compact to focus on the most valuable business insights.

---

## Page 1 — Executive Overview

### 🎯 Purpose

The Executive Overview provides a high-level view of macro business performance, revenue dynamics, product categories, payment adoption, and geographic revenue distribution.

It is designed to answer:

> **How is the business performing overall?**

### 🔎 Slicers

The page includes:

- Year
- Month
- Customer State
- Product Category

These filters allow users to dynamically analyze different periods, geographic segments, and product categories.

### 📌 KPI Metrics

**Total Revenue**
Total gross order value generated by the business. The metric is displayed in **MAD** following the Moroccanization of the original dataset.

**Total Orders**
Total number of transactions/orders included in the analysis.

**Average Order Value (AOV)**
Average revenue generated per order.

```text
AOV = Total Revenue / Total Orders
```

**Total Customers**
Number of unique customers represented in the dataset.

### 📈 Core Visuals

**Monthly Revenue Trend**
A line chart displaying revenue evolution over time. The month dimension is sorted chronologically:

```
January → February → March → ... → November → December
```

This allows users to identify revenue growth, declines, and seasonal patterns.

**Top Categories by Revenue**
A horizontal bar chart highlighting the product categories generating the highest revenue. This helps identify the most commercially important product categories.

**Payment Method Mix**
A donut chart showing the distribution of revenue across payment methods. This provides a quick overview of customer payment preferences.

**State Revenue Performance**
A geographic visualization comparing revenue across Moroccanized customer regions. This identifies the regions generating the highest revenue and provides a geographic perspective on business performance.

## Page 2 — Logistics & Delivery Performance

### 🎯 Purpose

The Logistics & Delivery page focuses on operational efficiency, fulfillment performance, regional delivery differences, and potential logistics bottlenecks.

It is designed to answer:

> **Where and when are delivery performance issues occurring?**

### 🔎 Slicers

The page includes:

- Year
- Month
- Seller State
- Delivery Status

These filters allow users to investigate logistics performance for specific periods, seller regions, and delivery outcomes.

### 📌 KPI Metrics

**Average Shipping Time — 2.3 days**
Represents the average time between order purchase and carrier handover.

```dax
Average Shipping Time =
AVERAGE(vw_sales[shipping_time_days])
```

**Average Delivery Time — 12 days**
Represents the average total duration between order purchase and customer delivery.

```dax
Average Delivery Time =
AVERAGE(vw_sales[delivery_time_days])
```

**Average Delivery Delay — -12 days**
Represents the average difference between the estimated delivery date and the actual delivery date.

```dax
Average Delivery Delay =
AVERAGE(vw_sales[delivery_delay_days])
```

A negative value indicates that delivery occurred earlier than the estimated delivery date, according to the project's delay definition.

**On-Time Delivery Rate — 93.6%**
Percentage of orders delivered on or before the estimated delivery date.

```dax
On-Time Delivery Rate =
DIVIDE(
    COUNTROWS(
        FILTER(
            vw_sales,
            vw_sales[delivery_delay_days] <= 0
        )
    ),
    COUNTROWS(vw_sales),
    0
)
```

### 📈 Core Visuals

**Monthly Delivery Volume & Status**
A stacked column chart breaking down monthly order volume by delivery status:

- Early
- On Time
- Late

This makes it possible to identify changes in delivery performance over time.

**Average Delay by Seller State**
A horizontal bar chart comparing average delivery delay across seller regions. This helps identify geographic differences in fulfillment performance.

**Shipping vs. Delivery Time Trend**
A dual-line chart comparing shipping time and total delivery time over time. This helps distinguish between the initial shipping/handover stage and the complete delivery journey.

**Average Delivery Time by Destination State**
A clustered bar chart comparing delivery duration across customer regions. The analysis highlights differences between regions such as:

- Dakhla-Oued Ed-Dahab
- Guelmim-Oued Noun
- Casablanca-Settat

This provides a geographic view of delivery lead-time variation.

**Customer Rating Impact by Delivery Status**
A column chart comparing average review scores across delivery statuses. The analysis shows a substantial difference between customers receiving early/on-time deliveries and customers experiencing late deliveries.

Average ratings decrease from approximately:

```
Early / On Time   ~4.0 ★
        ↓
Late              ~2.2 ★
```

This creates a direct connection between logistics performance and customer satisfaction.

## Page 3 — Customer & Payment Insights

### 🎯 Purpose

The Customer & Payment page focuses on payment adoption, installment behavior, customer distribution, and customer sentiment.

It is designed to answer:

> **How are customers paying, behaving, and responding to their experience?**

### 🔎 Slicers

The page includes:

- Year
- Month
- Customer State
- Payment Type

### 📌 KPI Metrics

**Total Customers**
Approximately 95K unique customers.

**Average Review Score**
Approximately 4.0 ★ average customer rating.

**Would Recommend Rate**
Approximately 75.0% of customers would recommend the service.

```dax
Recommend Rate =
DIVIDE(
    CALCULATE(
        COUNTROWS(vw_sales),
        vw_sales[would_recommend] = TRUE()
    ),
    COUNTROWS(vw_sales),
    0
)
```

**Cash on Delivery Rate**
Approximately 20.3% of orders use Cash on Delivery.

```dax
COD Rate =
DIVIDE(
    CALCULATE(
        COUNTROWS(vw_sales),
        vw_sales[cod_flag] = TRUE()
    ),
    COUNTROWS(vw_sales),
    0
)
```

### 📈 Core Visuals

**Payment Method Distribution**
A donut chart showing transaction volume by payment method. Bank Card represents approximately 74.8% of transaction volume, while Cash on Delivery represents approximately 20%. This highlights the dominance of electronic payment while showing that COD remains an important secondary payment channel.

**Order Volume by Installment Plan**
A clustered column chart categorizing orders according to installment tiers:

- 1x
- 2–3x
- 4–6x
- 7+

This provides insight into customer financing behavior.

**Customer Review Sentiment Breakdown**
A column chart categorizing customer feedback into:

- Positive
- Neutral
- Negative

This provides a simplified overview of customer sentiment.

**Top 10 Destination States by Revenue**
A horizontal bar chart displaying the ten customer regions generating the highest revenue. The leading regions include:

- Casablanca-Settat
- Rabat-Salé-Kénitra

This complements the seller-side geographic analysis presented on the Executive Overview page.

**Average Order Value by Payment Method**
A horizontal bar chart comparing average order value across payment methods. This allows users to identify differences in basket size associated with different payment behaviors.

## 💡 Key Business Takeaways

### 1. Logistics Drives Customer Satisfaction

Delivery performance has a strong relationship with customer satisfaction. Average review scores fall substantially for late deliveries, decreasing from approximately:

```
4.0 ★ → 2.2 ★
```

This suggests that delivery reliability is an important component of the customer experience.

### 2. Geographic Delivery Variation

Delivery performance varies considerably across Moroccanized regions.

Southern regions such as:

- Dakhla-Oued Ed-Dahab
- Guelmim-Oued Noun

show longer average delivery times than major commercial hubs such as Casablanca-Settat.

Some southern regions exceed 20 days of average delivery time, compared with approximately 8 days in Casablanca-Settat. This highlights potential geographic logistics constraints.

### 3. Payment Preferences

Bank Card is the dominant payment method, representing approximately 74.8% of transaction volume. Cash on Delivery remains an important secondary channel at approximately 20.3%.

The dashboard also shows differences in average basket size between payment methods, providing additional insight into customer purchasing behavior.

## 🏗️ Power BI Architecture

The Power BI dashboard represents the Business Intelligence layer of the overall ChriLy architecture.

```
Original Olist Dataset
        ↓
Data Quality & Cleaning
        ↓
Moroccanization
        ↓
PostgreSQL
        ↓
Data Warehouse
        ↓
Analytical Views
        ↓
Power BI
        ↓
Interactive Dashboard
```

The dashboard uses curated PostgreSQL analytical views rather than directly connecting to the original raw CSV files.

Where required, information is sourced from the appropriate analytical view. For example:

| Information            | Source            |
|-------------------------|--------------------|
| Customer unique ID      | Customer view      |
| Delivery time           | Delivery view      |
| Sales/order metrics      | Sales view         |

This keeps the analytical layer organized while avoiding unnecessary duplication of fields.

The dashboard was intentionally limited to three pages to keep the analysis focused and avoid unnecessary visual complexity.
